# Three research extensions — working note

3 October 2026. Not a public snapshot. Not an épreuve contradictoire. Not a closed result.

The core Pacte can be communicated with these three left open. Public text: `/documents/questions-ouvertes` `2026-10-03-2`.

## 1. Eurosystem — role, not a guarantee

Do not frame an ECB guarantee of Pacte mortgages.

Three distinct roles:

1. Supervision / shared macroprudential oversight. Borrower-level caps remain mainly national.
2. Liquidity / collateral. Eurosystem credit only against eligible assets and haircuts. Individual credit claims are in the framework. From 30 November 2026, haircuts on those claims take **amortisation type** into account (ECB/2026/27; press 17 Nov 2025, 29 Sep 2026). Non-amortising claims get higher haircuts. Relevant to IO; does **not** establish eligibility of the Pacte book.
3. Exceptional / ACC frameworks. Residential mortgages have been the largest contributor to additional collateral in some national ACC arrangements (already noted, open, in EC-05). Not a Pacte precedent.

Open question (French, public): quel rôle l’Eurosystème pourrait-il jouer dans la supervision prudentielle, l’éligibilité du refinancement et la liquidité du dispositif, **sans** transférer le risque de crédit des ménages à la banque centrale ?

## 2. Societal impact — five unsigned channels

Missing layer between €223bn cash-flow and “nouveau pacte social”. Not a GDP object.

Channels (sign not claimed): intergenerational transfers; retirement; housing mobility; inequality (owners vs renters); inheritance/debt norms.

Read by archetype, not averages: 35 first-time buyer; 50 established owner; 65 retiring owner; 75 mortgage-free owner; renter; heir.

## 3. Netherlands — first check, now partly sourced

DNB press 3 Mar 2026: IO is still **almost 40% of mortgage debt** (*hypotheekschuld*), not merely a loan-count. Average LTV just above 50%. IO loans: 7% LTV > 75% vs ~20% of the whole book. NPL 0.8%. First-time buyers: more than half LTV > 90%.

CBS 1 Jan 2024* (provisional): eigen woning €2 180.7bn; mortgage on that dwelling €807.5bn → **37.0%** consolidé agrégé on the same vintage.

Illustration, not a DNB statistic: 40% × 807.5 / 2 180.7 ≈ **14.8%**. Mixing DNB €890bn end-2025 with CBS 2024 housing ≈ 16%. Neither is IO-collateral-only.

Stress 2008–2013: ~20% nominal house-price fall; ~30% of mortgages underwater in 2013 (DNB LLD); four largest banks’ mortgage losses ~0.2% of book (DNB 2016). Does not prove the Pacte. French loss channel still unwired.
