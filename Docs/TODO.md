# France 2040 — to-do

28 September 2026. Working list, in the order below. The public site is not this list.

## Decision in force

`Pacte_du_Bilan_Francais_V2_enrichie.docx` is the reference document. The thesis is frozen. No major conceptual expansion until its assumptions have been attacked.

The argument may change only through the Decision Log (`06_Pacte_Decision_Log.docx`), not by rewriting the story each week. New figures stay marked as fact, hypothesis, scenario, or objective, as that log already requires.

The page at `/pacte` is a first French public draft taken from `01_Pacte_du_Bilan_Francais_Paper_v1.docx`. It is not the reference. Bringing it into line with V2 is packaging work, not a new idea.

## Order of work

1. **Freeze the thesis.** Done as a decision, above. V2 is the reference. Keep the Decision Log so the argument does not drift.
2. **Build the quantitative model.** Largest piece of work. A 2027–2040 model linking mortgage-equity extraction → household use of funds → domestic demand, imports and savings → investment and supply → employment → nominal and real GDP → tax receipts → expenditure → deficit → public debt. Then Base / Low / High / Failure. The €500–700bn should become an output, or a tested scenario, not an assertion. Sketch only: `08_Pacte_Modele_Macro.xlsx`.
3. **Build the consolidated French balance sheet.** Public debt and assets; household financial and real-estate assets and debt; corporate balance sheet; banking system; external position. The question: is France short of wealth, or is part of the problem where leverage and assets sit? Aggregates exist in V2 and in `04_Pacte_Evidence_Book.docx`. No consolidated statement yet.
4. **Household distribution.** The €8.85tn aggregate is not enough. Who owns the equity, by age, income, property value, geography and existing mortgage? How much could qualify at 40% and at 50% consolidated LTV and still pass an interest-service test? That is the addressable reservoir. Not started. Flagged as a priority test in the Evidence Book.
5. **International precedents.** UK equity release and lifetime mortgages, Dutch interest-only mortgages, Denmark, Switzerland, US HELOC and cash-out refinancing, Australia, and others. Evidence on consumption, defaults, retirement debt, house prices and regulation. Not a template for France. Listed as still to document in the Evidence Book. Not started.
6. **French hypothèque rechargeable, 2006–2014.** Why it did not scale: demand, distribution, regulation, pricing, consumer protection, banking incentives, or the wrong product. V2, section XXI, only notes that it was introduced in 2006 and repealed for households in 2014. No deep dive.
7. **Banking plumbing.** Who would fund €500bn+ of extra long-duration fixed-rate mortgages: deposits, covered bonds, securitisation, ECB-eligible collateral? Capital, liquidity, duration risk. Can the French banking system carry the asset expansion? Open questions only, in `03_Pacte_DFMA.docx` and the Decision Log.
8. **Fiscal side and the Pacte Spread.** A credible baseline without the Pacte: nominal GDP, public spending, tax receipts, interest, primary balance, debt/GDP. Then the overlay. The question is how much fiscal consolidation becomes possible without an equivalent demand shock. The Spread is defined. It is not yet quantified on a full baseline.
9. **Housing, treated as a central objection.** More borrowing capacity for existing owners can make housing less affordable for younger households. Need construction elasticity, land constraints, price-to-income ratios, first-time buyers, renters, downsizing. The Pacte needs a housing compact, not a paragraph of safeguards. Not started.
10. **Four contracts, specified.** Patrimonial and financial; fiscal; productive; employment and security of income. For each: obligations, metrics, thresholds, governance, and what happens if the other side is not delivered. Sketch only: `05_Pacte_Contrat_Social.docx`.
11. **Red-team document.** “25 reasons why the Pacte du Bilan Français could be a terrible idea.” Housing bubble, regressive transfer, Ricardian effects, low MPC among wealthy owners, imports, inflation, bank concentration, fiscal moral hazard, EU constraints, retirees spending their buffer, political capture, and the rest. Each objection marked evidence, unresolved, or potentially fatal. No advocacy. Not started.
12. **Legal and EU feasibility.** HCSF, ACPR, Banque de France, ECB/SSM, CRR/CRD, consumer-credit and mortgage rules, covered bonds, French succession and mortgage law, EU fiscal framework. Not a treatise. A map of what would make the architecture impossible or materially different. Open in the Decision Log. Not started.
13. **Public model.** The spreadsheet becomes downloadable from France2040.eu. A reader can change LTV, adoption, extraction, MPC, import leakage, real growth, inflation, spending growth, interest rates, and see the thesis hold or fail. Not started. Depends on item 2.
14. **France2040.eu, kept small.** Possible first pages: the hypothesis, the paper, the model, evidence, critique, about. No movement, membership, manifesto, or party branding. It should look like independent economic research. A local shell exists (home, French pact draft, documents, project). It is not this site yet, and it is not deployed.
15. **Three versions of the argument.** A 30–40 page reference paper; a 4–6 page policy brief; a one-page executive note. V2 is the long narrative reference. Paper v1 is shorter than the reference paper described here. No brief and no one-page note yet.
16. **First ten red-team readers.** Not publicity. A macroeconomist, a housing economist, a former or current banker, a public-finance specialist, an actuary, a mortgage specialist, a demographer, an EU or regulatory person, a labour economist, and someone hostile to the proposal. The question is where it breaks. Not started. `07_Pacte_Strategie_Deploiement.docx` describes a later contact sequence; it is not this step.
17. **V3 only after that.** Record every serious objection, change the model, rewrite the paper, publish what remains unresolved. If something kills the thesis, that is a result. Blocked on items 2–16.
18. **Public dissemination only after V3.** Open paper and model, invite academic and institutional critique, then approach policy institutions, parliamentary committees and journalists broadly. Not around persuading one political camp. Blocked on item 17.

## What to do next

Three workstreams, not eighteen sequential gates.

**A — Quantify it.** Consolidated balance sheet, household equity distribution, and the full macro model. Items 2, 3, 4 and 8.

**B — Try to kill it.** International evidence, the hypothèque rechargeable, banking feasibility, housing consequences, and the red-team paper. Items 5, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12.

**C — Package it.** France2040.eu, a five-page brief, and the public model. Items 13, 14 and 15. The public model waits on A. The site stays small until there is something to publish that can fail in public.

Items 1, 10, 16, 17 and 18 sit around those streams: the freeze is in force, the four contracts get specified as the model and the red team produce thresholds, the ten readers come before V3, and dissemination comes last.
