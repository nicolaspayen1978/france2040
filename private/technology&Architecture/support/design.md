# Support link

1 October 2026. Phase 0 built.

The public site carries one discreet support invitation in the footer. The visible label is
**Soutenir France 2040** and the destination is the owner-provided Stripe-hosted payment page.

The site does not embed checkout, collect payment data, state that a contribution is tax
deductible, or represent support as part of the research evidence. The external page opens in a
new tab and its accessible name identifies Stripe as the payment destination.

There is no dedicated support route in this slice. The research content and publication snapshots
are unchanged.
