# Support link

1 October 2026. Phase 0 built.

The public site carries one discreet support invitation in the footer. The visible label is
**Soutenir France 2040** and its destination is `/soutenir`. That page explains what the voluntary
contribution supports, says that it does not qualify for a tax reduction, and then links to the
owner-provided Stripe-hosted payment page.

The site does not embed checkout, collect payment data, state that a contribution is tax
deductible, or represent support as part of the research evidence. Only the Stripe call to action
opens a new tab, and its accessible name identifies Stripe as the payment destination.

The support page is included in the sitemap. The research content and publication snapshots are
unchanged.
